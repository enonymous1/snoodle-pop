How to play

  • Aim with the mouse, arrow keys, or your finger. The dotted line shows the bounce off the side walls.
  • Shoot by clicking, pressing Space / ↑, or lifting your finger. Match 3 or more of the same colour to pop them.
  • Drop clusters: bubbles no longer connected to the top fall for bonus points.
  • Don't let the board cross the red danger line. Every 6 shots a new row pushes down from the top.
  • Clear the whole board to advance a level — more rows, more colours.
  • Swap with the ⇄ button or S when the next bubble is more useful.

Inspired by the classic bubble shooter Snood (1996). All characters, colours, sounds and code here are original — this is a fan-style tribute, not Snood itself, and it uses no Snood art, audio or assets.
